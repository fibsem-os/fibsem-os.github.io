# Your first lamella

The whole path from a connected microscope to a polished lamella, in one
run, on the simulator or on an instrument. Each step links to the page that
explains it; read those when a step needs more than a line.

## Before you start

- fibsemOS is [installed](/docs/installation) and
  [connected](/docs/getting-started). On a laptop, use the simulator.
- On an instrument: the grid is loaded, the beams are on, and you have
  taken an [image](/docs/imaging) of it.

## 1. Create an experiment

**Create Experiment** at the top right. Name it, pick the experiments
folder, leave the default on-grid protocol selected, and create. The
AutoLamella tabs wake up. [Experiments](/docs/experiments)

![Create Experiment](/doc/img/experiments/create-experiment.png)

## 2. Image the grid

Move to the SEM orientation and put the surface in coincidence
([Movement](/docs/movement)). On the **Overview** tab, choose the electron
beam, 3 × 3 tiles at 400 µm, and **Acquire Overview**.
[Overview acquisition](/docs/overview)

![The SEM overview](/doc/img/overview/sem-overview.png)

## 3. Mark the lamellae

Right-click a good square on the overview and choose **Add New Position
Here**. Two or three positions is plenty for a first run.
[Overview acquisition](/docs/overview#3-mark-lamella-positions)

![Positions marked](/doc/img/overview/positions-marked.png)

## 4. Check the protocol

On the **Protocol** tab, look at Rough Milling and Polishing: the currents
and the milling angle should be ones your instrument uses. On the
simulator, leave everything as it is. [Protocols](/docs/protocols)

## 5. Run the first two tasks, supervised

On the **Workflow** tab tick your lamellae and the first two tasks, **Setup
Lamella Position** and **Mill Fiducial**, leave both supervised, and press
**Run Workflow**. Confirm the preflight. [Workflows](/docs/workflows)

![Workflow tab, ready to run](/doc/img/workflows/workflow-tab.png)

When **Attention Required** lights up, go to the Experiment tab and
answer: **Tilt** to the milling angle, double-click the feature to centre
it and **Continue**, then **Run Milling** for the fiducial and **Continue**
once you are happy with it. Each lamella in turn.

![A question during the run](/doc/img/workflows/prompt-confirm.png)

## 6. Rough milling and polishing

Back on the Workflow tab, tick **Rough Milling** and **Polishing**, and
run. Supervised the first time: at each milling prompt check the patterns
sit where you want on the FIB view, then **Run Milling** and **Continue**.
On the simulator each stage takes a few seconds; on an instrument, minutes
to tens of minutes per lamella.

![Milling prompt](/doc/img/workflows/prompt-runmillingtask.png)

## 7. Look at what you made

The summary at the end lists every task and its outcome. The
**Lamella** tab's Review shows each lamella's images task by task, and the
FIB view shows the finished lamella. [Lamella](/docs/lamella)

![The workflow summary](/doc/img/workflows/workflow-summary.png)

## Next time

Turn supervision off for Rough Milling and Polishing, and let the run go
unattended. The [Tasks](/docs/tasks) page says what each task does and
which to keep an eye on.
